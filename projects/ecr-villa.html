ECR Villa

Villa & Landscape

Project Story

ECR Villa – A Home Inspired by Nature, Tradition & the Sea This villa is designed to create a warm, timeless living experience by blending traditional architecture with contemporary interiors. Rich wooden finishes, natural stone textures, and handcrafted details bring character to every space while maintaining a clean and elegant aesthetic. Each room has been thoughtfully curated with its own unique theme. The master bedroom embraces traditional elements with warm earthy tones and handcrafted décor, while the guest bedroom draws inspiration from the sea, featuring calming blue hues and coastal-inspired accents for a refreshing and relaxing atmosphere. The custom bar, spacious dining area, and functional kitchen are designed for both everyday comfort and entertaining. The bathrooms continue the design story with distinctive finishes, creating spaces that are practical, stylish, and serene. The result is a villa that celebrates craftsmanship, personalized design, and a seamless balance between luxury, comfort, and nature.
